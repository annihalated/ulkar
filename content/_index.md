title: Music and Social Order
description: An intimate salon with writer and composer Ulkar Aghayeva, in New York City on 17 October at 6pm.
---

Born and raised in Baku, Ulkar trained in biochemistry and conducted doctoral research in developmental neuroscience at Columbia University. She currently works as a science writer with articles appearing in [Asimov Press](https://press.asimov.com/articles/insect-diapause), [Works in Progress](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/waking-up-sciences-sleeping-beauties/), and her science history blog, [Measure for Measure](https://measureformeasure.co/). Ulkar is interested in ideas surrounding scientific creativity, [experimental beauty](https://press.asimov.com/articles/beautiful-experiments), method and the [role of theory](https://press.asimov.com/articles/theory-biology) in science. Outside of writing, she is a trained pianist and [composes works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uls7oBPaRfo) that explore emergent musical patterns at the boundaries between tradition and new forms.

In an intimate salon in New York City, she will be giving a talk on [Music and Social Order](https://ulkaraghayeva.substack.com/p/music-as-a-power-maintaining-and?r=7e9vn&utm_medium=ios) – looking into how music can foster harmony and cohesion while also fueling political upheaval. By drawing on ancient Greek and Chinese history through to modernity, Ulkar considers how, in music, the personal is political.

We will also be joined by a Renaissance lutenist and a baritone for a living-room performance before engaging in a salon style discussion.

We would be delighted to have you join us.
